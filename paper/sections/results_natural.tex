For the six accepted families the recipe was fixed and logged before any natural agent ran (decision log D64, D65): fifteen prompt templates with their hashes, the order of work with its freeze points, and the rendered inputs and proposer prompts of each family. Its first four phases have since run for all six families (D70). Each family now has an initial-only, a full-session and a reference contract, and its seven verifiers (C1--C4, B1, B2 and B3) are frozen; each phase end is a committed record. The reference checks, the independent labeler, the held-out pool and labeling have not run, so every natural result is \unfilled{pending: \NATURALRUN{} not yet run}.

\paragraph{What the construction already shows.} These are observations of the build, made by model agents and not reviewed by a person; they are not results. At the first audit, the natural reference fails 9 of the 12 audited verifiers. Those audits grade byte copies of the plain verifiers, so C1 fails its own family's reference in five of the six families and C3 in four (D70). A pull request's addressing commit is therefore often not a complete solution to the requirements recovered from its own thread: by the generators' account, a content type is left unconverted, a requirement's wording is not met, or the fix does not take effect in the task's environment. Because audit:v1 holds the reference to be valid, its revisers loosened checks toward the reference's behaviour. Five of the twelve audits still fail after three revisions, three of them on a development candidate that the revisers could not tell apart from the reference on its target requirement. For the natural arm, the reference is evidence about the correction rather than an oracle, and the run reports each family with these facts beside its numbers. With six tasks the arm is below the size rule of protocol~\S19.6, and its results, once they exist, are reported per family as case studies.
