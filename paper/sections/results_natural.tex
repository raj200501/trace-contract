The natural arm has a recorded funnel but no verifier result yet. From \nat{aidev.s1.prs} agent-authored PRs in \nat{aidev.s1.repos} repositories, the deterministic stages S1--S8a keep \nat{aidev.s8a.comments} thread-starting human review comments on \nat{aidev.s8a.prs} PRs in \nat{aidev.s8a.repos} repositories (Table~\ref{tab:natural-funnel}). Two configuration-A judges then read each sampled comment with the diff to the first later commit that touches its file (S9). In the first sample, \nat{s9.first.qualifying} of \nat{s9.first.comments} comments qualify (\nat{s9.first.rate}, Wilson \nat{s9.first.wilson}); in the extension, decided after the first sample's replay yield was known and before any natural contract, verifier or label existed (protocol~\S19), \nat{s9.ext.qualifying} of \nat{s9.ext.comments} qualify (\nat{s9.ext.rate}, Wilson \nat{s9.ext.wilson}). Both rates are model-judged. The most common reason a comment fails is that it asks for no behaviour change or defect fix (\nat{s9.first.fail.behaviour} and \nat{s9.ext.fail.behaviour} comments).

The extension's \nat{fam.ext} families await replay. For the six accepted families the recipe is fixed and logged before any natural agent ran (decision log D64, D65): fifteen prompt templates with their hashes, the order of work with its freeze points, and the rendered inputs and proposer prompts of each family. Contract recovery, verifier generation, the held-out pool and labeling have not run, so every natural result is \unfilled{pending: \NATURALRUN{} not yet run}. With six tasks the arm is below the size rule of protocol~\S19.6, and its results, once they exist, are reported per family as case studies.
