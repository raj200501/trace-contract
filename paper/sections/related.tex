\paragraph{Trace-derived evaluation.} Trace2Eval reconstructs evaluation instances (a distilled request, a recovered workspace and a containerised environment) from consented online coding-agent sessions and scores artifacts with a calibrated rubric judge \cite{trace2eval}. SWE-Together curates repository-level tasks from recorded sessions, including SWE-chat, with a replaying user simulator, deterministic gates and a rubric judge, and argues that execution-only scoring can miss requirements introduced later in the interaction \cite{swetogether}. Neither reports verifier false-acceptance or false-rejection rates against independently labeled candidate patches (\S\ref{sec:intro}).

\paragraph{Real developer--agent sessions.} SWE-chat collects real coding-agent sessions from public repositories, with LLM-annotated prompt pushback and links to commits \cite{swechat}.
