# Manuscript draft

`main.tex` is a working draft. Build it from this directory with `latexmk -pdf main.tex` (needs `biber`); send the
console output to a file of another name than `main.out`, which hyperref writes. Regenerate the tables, numbers and
figures first (the commands below and the top-level README).

The draft's earlier sections (`data.tex`, `method.tex`, `protocol.tex`, `results*.tex`, `analysis.tex`,
`limitations.tex`, `appendix.tex`) were retired after the move-map check: every paragraph the plan keeps was found in
its destination, with its decision-log entries, protocol sections, names, number macros and numbers.

Rules for this directory:

* Every number in the text is a macro of a generated `numbers.tex` (`\fv`, `\nat`, `\nrv`, `\plv`, `\dv`), whose value
  an evidence ledger lists with the file and field it comes from; dates and times in prose too. Every number in
  `generated/*.tex` and its subdirectories is produced by `make_tables.py` or `make_figures.py` from recorded files.
  `check_numbers.py` reports any number outside a macro (below).
* `references.bib` contains only entries verified against primary sources (date of check in the file header). The
  literature that no verified entry covers yet is named as a gap in the threats to validity, without a citation.
* The posted PDF holds no `UNFILLED` marker, no undefined reference and no `TODO` but the author line, which stays a TODO
  for the human authors, as the working-draft subtitle stays until posting (the owner's defaults).
* No model identifier, agent-tool name or product name. The paper says what the records name and no more anonymity than
  they keep: the protocol and the decision log name the agent tool and define configuration A as the build session's
  default model and B as the smallest model tier the tool offers, of one model family of one provider; the commit
  messages' co-author lines and the committed controlled-session transcripts carry model names; the paper names none
  (Section 2.1; the AI-assistance statement says "an AI coding agent"). The natural template keeps its stricter rule:
  no tier wording (`tests/test_natural_template.py`).

Tables and figures of the later runs (decision log D66):

* `make_tables.py` writes the pilot's tables to `generated/*.tex` and `evidence_ledger.md` (the top-level README's
  command, which `tests/test_make_tables.py` checks byte for byte). Its other modes write their own subdirectories and
  ledgers and leave the pilot's untouched:
  `.venv/bin/python paper/make_tables.py --fresh results/runs/fresh-v1 --regrade results/runs/pilot-v0.3.5-regrade --natural`
  writes `generated/fresh/` with `evidence_ledger_fresh.md` (fresh-v1 and the 0.3.5 re-grade) and `generated/natural/`
  with `evidence_ledger_natural.md` (the natural arm's funnel, S10 and the Trace Commons screen). The extension's S10
  was not run (D66, D75), and the funnel and S10 tables say so instead of printing a pending marker
  (`tests/test_make_tables_natural.py`).
* `.venv/bin/python paper/make_figures.py results/runs/fresh-v1` draws the figures with TikZ (no matplotlib) into
  `generated/figures/`, each with its source `.tikz`, a `.json` of every plotted value or stated number and its source,
  and `evidence_ledger_figures.md`: `fresh_fa_fr_by_family` (Fig. 2, `fig:fresh-fa` and `fig:fresh-fr`: false acceptance
  and false rejection per family under D19 in one figure; the filled marks are the correction violators accepted, a
  family without correction violators says so and shows its regression-only units as hollow marks, and a condition that
  fails a family's reference is annotated there) and `design_example` (Fig. 1, `fig:design`: the design and the CS-04
  and CS-01 examples, whose excerpts are exact substrings of the scenario files and whose numbers are the `\fv` values).
  One invocation writes both and deletes any other file of the directory.
* `generated/fresh/numbers.tex` and `generated/natural/numbers.tex` define number macros, used in the text as `\fv{key}`
  and `\nat{key}`; an undefined key prints as `UNFILLED`. Each value is in its ledger with its file and field.
  `--fresh` also writes the correction violators' distinct outcome patterns per family (`cv.patterns.<family>`,
  and `cv.patterns`, their sum, each pattern counted within its family), the behaviours table (`fresh_behaviours.tex`,
  `tab:fresh-behaviours`), the primary per-family
  table split into false acceptance and false rejection with rule D19's rows only (`tab:fresh-family`) and the second
  labeler's view apart (`fresh_per_family_labeler.tex`, `tab:fresh-family-labeler`), and the comparisons in a compact
  table (`tab:fresh-comparisons`) and in full (`fresh_comparisons_full.tex`, `tab:fresh-comparisons-full`). Every caption
  of fresh-v1's tables and figures ends with one marker, "◇ Automated pilot; model-proposed labels, no human labels; <n>
  controlled families; descriptive" (a diamond, since the natural false-acceptance table marks with a dagger the cells
  the oracle-gated view drops), with <n> read from the run's analysis, and names the conditions as the
  restructured text does (initial-only C1 and C2, correction-aware C3 and C4, the session-LLM baseline B2). The pilot's
  own tables (`generated/*.tex`, `evidence_ledger.md`) are frozen at `c5ede4c` (`scripts/verify_frozen_inputs.py`,
  check e) and keep the names and captions they were frozen with.
* `.venv/bin/python paper/make_tables.py --pilot-numbers results/runs/pilot-v0.3.3` writes `generated/pilot/numbers.tex`
  (`\plv{key}`) and `evidence_ledger_pilot.md` from the frozen pilot run, its comparison files, the B1 check-origin files
  and verifier specs, and the exploratory re-analysis of D49 (`results/analyses/pilot-v0.3.3-exploratory/`, required);
  it writes no pilot table.
* `.venv/bin/python paper/make_tables.py --design` writes `generated/design/numbers.tex` (`\dv{key}`), the amendments
  table (`amendments_table.tex`, `tab:amendments`) and `evidence_ledger_design.md` from `design_constants.json`. Each
  constant there names the checks that tie its value to its source (a quote that must occur in the cited file and state
  the value, a code pattern, a count of files, or a field of recorded JSON files reduced to one value), and the protocol's
  versions are recomputed from the git objects of their commits (v0.1 to v0.5; the mode fails without the repository's
  history) or from byte prefixes of the current protocol, each hash and hash time as its decision-log entry records it.
  A value whose check fails is never written. The table's text is authored there and holds no number of its own. The
  constants include the appendices' counts and times that no run analysis holds, each quoted from the decision log,
  the protocol, the annotation guide or a fresh-attempt audit record, or read from the code (e.g. `audit.py`'s
  `NAMED_IN_MESSAGE`).
* `main.tex` defines `\fv`, `\nat`, `\nrv`, `\plv` and `\dv` alike and inputs each namespace's `numbers.tex` where it
  exists. `check_numbers.py` reports every digit and every count written as a word (zero to twenty, thirty to ninety,
  hundred, half, dozen; single, once, twice, thrice and "a pair of") outside a number macro in `main.tex`'s title and body and the sections it inputs, and every macro
  whose key no generated `numbers.tex` defines. Exempt: identifiers (families, conditions, turns and variants, stages,
  phases, decision-log entries, requirement ids, candidate, run and template ids), protocol sections and version names,
  the arguments of `\ref`, `\label`, `\cite` and `\input`, LaTeX lengths and column specifications, file names, paths and
  hashes in `\texttt`, and the phrases "one model family of one provider", "one-sided", "two-sided", "single-pass",
  "single-turn", "once" as a conjunction and the counting rule "counts once"; dates and times in prose are not. `tests/test_check_numbers.py` requires every section `main.tex` inputs to pass, the main text
  (`MAIN_TEXT`, in the plan's order) and then the appendices (`APPENDICES`, A to I), and the retired sections to be
  gone (`RETIRED`). `scripts/check_abstract.py` expands the five namespaces from the generated files before it counts
  the abstract's characters (limit 1,900), and a key that is not generated is a problem.
* The natural run (protocol §20): once `results/runs/natural-pr-v1` exists (as `scripts/publish_natural_run.py` publishes
  it), `.venv/bin/python paper/make_tables.py --natural-run results/runs/natural-pr-v1` writes `generated/natural_run/`
  with `evidence_ledger_natural_run.md`, and the macros `\nrv{key}` in `generated/natural_run/numbers.tex` (`main.tex`
  defines `\nrv` as it defines `\fv` and `\nat`, and inputs that file where it exists). Its rules:
  * It reads the run's `analysis.json` and `manifest.json` (the run's id, times, code revision, repeats, pilot flag and
    invocations), and for its privacy check the agent product names of the AIDev screening record
    (`data/manifests/screening_natural_aidev.json`); nothing else. An analysis that is
    not a natural run's, or that is not in case-study mode, is refused. Each run is reported on its own (§20.3), so the
    size rule counts the run's own accepted tasks, never the committed index's over both samples: with 10 or more, §15.6
    and §15.7 apply in full, and their pooled tables are not this mode's. An analysis made before that rule (with no
    `meta.case_study.accepted_in_run`) is refused too.
  * Six per-family tables: false acceptance and false rejection per condition under D19 (C1 to C4, B2, B1, and B3 apart
    as the secondary arm; a cell the oracle-gated view drops is marked); which conditions accept each family's pre-review
    state under the source-evidence label (marked as that view marks them), with its D19 and second labels; the sanity
    grading, each condition's outcome on the family's reference solution and on its no-op (`sanity_per_family`; the
    marker and these outcomes must agree); the second labeling (the labeler's status, the labels, the agreement with D19,
    apart for the pre-review states and the pool); the construction facts (contract counts, checks per verifier, the
    audits of C2 and C4, B3, B1, the reference checks' status record, cost); and the coverage of the reference contract's
    scored requirements by each recovered contract under each of the two mappers, with the reference contract's
    correction requirements and those each recovered contract leaves uncovered, and, as the controlled study's
    coverage table gives them, the recovered requirements that each mapper judges to contradict the reference contract
    and those each recovered contract marks superseded. The sanity and coverage tables are
    tables of their own in App. G: the table of the pre-review states is a main-text top float, whose height the layout
    bounds, and the construction facts fill their page.
  * Besides the tables' values, the macros give the run's facts (`run.date`, `run.started`, `run.finished` in UTC,
    `run.revision`, `run.harness`, `run.repeats`, `run.pilot`, `run.invocations`, and `run.families`, the run's own
    families from the analysis's `natural.tasks`) and, per family, D19's labels of its held-out candidates
    (`d19.<family>.valid`, `.invalid`, `.ambiguous`, `.unlabeled`, and `.ambiguous.ids` and `.ambiguous.reasons`: each
    ambiguous candidate with the category of D19's reason in brackets, which `tracecontract analyze` carries into the
    run's label block, `labels.<task>.d19`). An analysis without that block is refused. For statements about a family,
    `nd.<family>.reject` names the conditions that graded its reference solution, its no-op and its held-out candidates
    and passed none of them, `nd.<family>.accept` those that passed every one, and `noop.<family>.accepted` those that
    pass its no-op, each as a sentence lists names (`none` for no condition).
  * Nothing is pooled over the families: no pooled rate, interval or p-value, and no total.
  * Every caption ends with the marker "‡ Natural case study; caveats in Section~\ref{sec:natural-cases}": the caveats
    are stated once, at the head of the natural section.
  * Every table cell is a ledger entry `<row> | <family> = <cell>` that names the `analysis.json` fields it is made from,
    each as `<dot path>=<JSON value>`; each field belongs to one family, or to the run. The numbers in the captions and
    the macros are entries too. `tests/test_make_tables_natural_run.py` checks every cell against its fields.
  * Its outputs get the natural arm's privacy check, as `--natural`'s do; its digit-run rule skips the run's own code
    revisions, which are commits of this repository.
* `sections/natural.tex` is the template of the natural case-study section, fixed before any analysis of the run: every
  value in it is a `\nrv` or `\nat` macro, and it prints `UNFILLED` until `--natural-run` has run. `main.tex` inputs it as
  the natural section. After the analysis its per-family detail was moved unchanged to Appendix G
  (`sections/app_natural_cases.tex`, which `app_natural.tex` inputs; D80): the caveats on the
  build's process, the table of per-family caveats (`tab:nr-caveats`), the family paragraphs and the cost per family;
  in a later round, the status paragraph's sentence on the run's dates and invocations (D82).
  Both files are the template: every rule of its header holds for both, and `tests/test_natural_template.py` reads both.
  Outside them and the generated tables the template points only to the two places D77 left open until the restructure
  gave them their content, Appendix A's `tab:amendments` (its v0.12 row) and Appendix G's `tab:natural-vs-controlled`,
  and to the controlled study's coverage table (`tab:coverage`), to which the reading with the gate points
  (`CROSS_REFS`). The test checks that every key it uses is generated, that it compiles with `main.tex`'s preamble, its
  wording, the disclosures its records require, and that every decision-log entry it cites is logged or is the pending
  next entry its header declares. Its fixed
  sentences state construction facts that the analysis also computes; the test's `PREMISES` lists them, and
  `test_the_run_s_own_numbers_bear_out_the_template_s_premises` runs on `generated/natural_run/numbers.tex`: a premise
  that fails means the sentence is rewritten from the records. The closing paragraph's integration slot was replaced
  after `--natural-run` by the sentence its rule asks for (D79), and rewritten once the table of the pre-review states
  marked the cells the oracle-gated view drops (D80), within the scope the template fixed before the analysis (D77: one
  sentence for each pattern of the C1 and C3 cells of that table, each family named once and none counted);
  `test_the_integration_s_sentence_states_the_run_s_own_cells` checks each cell they state (`INTEGRATION`). The
  paragraph that reads the families with the reference gate was written after the analysis too (D80), outside the
  pre-commitment; `test_the_reading_with_the_gate_states_the_run_s_own_cells` checks each family statement it makes on the
  run's numbers and its published analysis (`READING_CELLS`, `READING_ORDER` and the coverage cells it names). The
  template's header lists what changed after the analysis, in D79's round, in D80's and in D82's.
* All labels behind these numbers are model proposals; no human labels exist. The natural run `natural-pr-v1` is
  published (`results/runs/natural-pr-v1`), and its tables and `\nrv` numbers are generated.
